Hangman Game

How to Play

  1. 01
    Guess a letter — Click any letter button on the keyboard, or press a key on your physical keyboard.
  2. 02
    Correct guess — The letter appears in every matching position in the hidden word.
  3. 03
    Wrong guess — A new body part is added to the hangman drawing. You only get 6 wrong guesses!
  4. 04
    Use the Hint — Click the Hint button to reveal the word's category and a clue. One hint per round.
  5. 05
    Win — Reveal every letter before the drawing is complete and you win! 🎉
  6. 06
    Lose — Six wrong guesses fills the gallows. The answer is revealed. Click Restart Game to try again.
💬 All words are CS & tech themed — great for developer interview prep!
